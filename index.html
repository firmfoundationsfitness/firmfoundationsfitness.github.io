Why we exist

More than a gym.
A place to grow. A place to experience freedom.

Firm Foundations' mission is to help our customers improve more than just their physical health. We exist to help people experience the physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual freedom that can only be found with Christ as the foundation of our lives.

We believe training is an act of stewardship—and community is where transformation takes root.
