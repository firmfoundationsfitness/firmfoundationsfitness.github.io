04 Important to know

Train wisely.
Stay grounded.

These online programs are general fitness education and are not a substitute for individualized medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or in-person coaching. Consider your current health, experience, and limitations before beginning.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting if you have a medical condition, injury, significant pain, or concerns about whether exercise is appropriate for you. Stop an exercise that causes sharp or unusual pain, and use the provided scaling options when a movement or workload is beyond your current ability.

Results vary. Consistency, recovery, nutrition, and individual circumstances all affect progress.
